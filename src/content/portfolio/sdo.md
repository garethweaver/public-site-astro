As a personal project for a friend I redesigned his osteopathy website twice,
once in 2015 and then again a decade later in 2025. The latest build and design
was put together in AstroJs and ES6.

The first pass done in 2015 was to give it a fresh feel and make it mobile safe
while modernising it. Markup was put together in middleman before being
integrated into Anchor CMS.
